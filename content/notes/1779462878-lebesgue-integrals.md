title: Lebesgue integrals
---

The Lebesgue integral consider the size of the set where a function takes certain values. 

Roughly speaking, the {{< backlink "1779462006-riemann-integral" "Riemann integral" >}} cuts up the domain into intervals, while the Lebesgue integral cuts up the range into values and asks how large the corresponding level sets are.
